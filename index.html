About Masruq

An agricultural processing company built on consistency.

We source, process and supply Nigerian agricultural products for the businesses and households that depend on them.

Our story

Founded to connect farms and markets.

Masruq Investment Limited was established in 2015 to take part in Nigeria's agricultural value chain — not only as a trader, but as a processor with a stake in the quality of what reaches the market.

Today we process and supply legumes, flours, spices and animal feed. Each category carries the same expectation: careful sourcing, consistent processing and dependable supply.

We work with households, retailers, distributors, manufacturers and food-service operators — different customers, one standard.

What guides us

Mission, vision and values.

Mission

To process and supply agricultural products that businesses and households can rely on — consistently, and at the volumes they need.

Vision

To be a trusted link between Nigeria's agricultural output and the market that depends on it.

Values
  • Integrity — we say what we can supply and supply what we say.
  • Consistency — repeatability matters more than one good batch.
  • Stewardship — we treat produce, people and partnerships with care.
  • Partnership — we build for long-term supply relationships.
Sourcing

Sourcing with the finished product in mind.

Quality begins before processing. We select raw agricultural produce with attention to variety, condition and suitability for its intended use — whether that is a retail pack of beans, a milled flour or a spice blend.

Working close to the source helps us understand what we are buying and plan supply around the realities of each season.
